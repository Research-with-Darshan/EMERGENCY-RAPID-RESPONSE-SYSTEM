Classification and retention

Classification endpoints process the five original coursework datasets when they are placed in data/source/.

Classification levels

PUBLIC — approved for broad release.

OPERATIONAL — necessary for authorised response activity.

SENSITIVE — personal, medical or similarly sensitive details.

RESTRICTED — intelligence or national-security indicators.

Automated rules are indicative only. Ambiguous and high-impact records require human review.

Retention demonstration

Restricted records use a configurable 20-year demonstration retention period. This is not legal advice or a statement of the actual statutory schedule.
